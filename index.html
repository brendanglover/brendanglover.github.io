I'm a vibe coder — and I say that with full confidence. I can't recite syntax from memory or whiteboard an algorithm under pressure. What I can do is look at a broken or inefficient process, understand it completely, and build something that fixes it — using every tool available to me, AI very much included.

At Incubeta, I manage product feed infrastructure for e-commerce clients across multiple markets — data sanitation, policy disapproval investigation, GMC governance, and custom transformer logic on Feedonomics. As part of the Marketing Intelligence team I build AI automations that free teams from tedious, time-consuming tasks so they can focus on the work that actually matters.

Right now I'm building a custom AI agent that analyses thousands of Helium 10 keywords and generates optimised Amazon backend keyword strings within the 249-byte limit — across multiple regions and languages. I'm also building client reporting databases and a NotebookLM knowledge base that trains AI agents on client-specific context.

Outside of work: building free tools at GapCalc and Zink., learning to weld properly, and teaching my daughter that the best way to understand something is to build it.
